# Cito.gg Sports and Esports Website

[Back to my portfolio](../README.md) · [Visit Cito.gg](https://cito.gg/) · [Visit Cito API](https://citoapi.com/)

## Explore the live website

Cito.gg is a consumer-facing sports and esports website. Visitors can explore a CS2 match center, schedules and results, tournament pages, team rankings, and player information. The site also links to UFC and tennis coverage.

## How it fits my work

At CITO Media LLC, my responsibilities include website development, cold outreach through email and messaging, promotional ads and campaign concepts, SEO, customer communication, and data validation.

The two live websites show different sides of the product experience:

- **Cito API** presents sports and esports data to developers building their own products.
- **Cito.gg** presents match and performance information to sports fans through a website they can explore directly.

## Areas I focus on

- Clear website content and product communication.
- Audience research and feedback that inform marketing decisions.
- Promotional concepts and outreach that introduce people to our work.
- Reviewing data and investigating discrepancies.

The websites are developing products. Features and coverage can change as work continues.
