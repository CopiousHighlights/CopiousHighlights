## The work

I manage sports accounts across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, with more than **50,000 combined followers**. That figure sums followers across accounts and platforms; it does not represent unique audience members.

My responsibilities include researching topics, managing publishing, and using platform performance data and audience feedback to guide content decisions.

## How I approach content

1. **Research the topic and audience.** Identify sports stories and moments that fit the account's focus.
2. **Choose a format.** Consider how to present the subject for the platform and the people watching.
3. **Publish and listen.** Manage publishing and pay attention to audience responses.
4. **Review performance.** Use available platform data and feedback to guide future topics, formats, and publishing choices.
